THE JIMBARAN HUB STORY

A contemporary village centre for connection and creativity.

A home for people and subcultures to connect, learn, collaborate, and grow together in South Bali.

More than a destination.

Jimbaran Grove is a contemporary village centre in South Kuta where people and subcultures connect, learn, and grow together. It brings together activities, events, food, sports, wellness, arts, education, and spaces for collaboration.

Its spirit is built around three everyday ideas: Work, Play, and Learn. A place to meet new people, discover interests, share ideas, and take part in a growing community.
